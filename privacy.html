Privacy Policy & Terms

Short version: CUTE-S AI uses outside services (Google Firebase and Puter). Your data does not stay only inside CUTE-S AI. Below is exactly where it goes.

Who processes your data

Google Firebase (login, database, file storage): your email or phone number, your saved chats, projects, saved items, study profile, and the notes you choose to publish.

Puter and the AI providers behind it: everything you send to the AI is processed by Puter and by the AI company that runs the chosen model. This includes your chat messages, the text read from files or photos you attach, photos you ask to edit, and image descriptions. We do not control how those services handle data; please read their own privacy policies. Do not send passwords, ID numbers, bank details or other secrets to the AI.

Wikipedia: when Search or Research is on, only your question words are sent to Wikipedia.

What is stored

Chats are stored in your account so you can continue on any device. Other users cannot read them. They are not end-to-end encrypted, which means the service provider (Google) and the app owner, through the Firebase console, are technically able to access stored data. We do not read private chats and we do not use them for AI training.

Files you attach to a chat: the text is used to answer you, but it is not saved in your chat history. Only the file name and your own question are kept.

Generated images are kept in this device's browser storage only, not uploaded by us. They stay on the device until you delete them or clear the browser data.

Notes you publish are visible to verified CUTE-S users, together with your name prefix. Do not publish anything private.

C-Univers-AI (image, file, video, coding, research and data tools)

These tools send your input through Puter to the AI model you choose, exactly like the chat. Video analysis cuts frames on your device and sends them for reading; sound is not analyzed. Spreadsheets are read and calculated on your device; the AI only receives the summary you can see before sending. Research searches Wikipedia, which receives your question words. Python and JavaScript you run in the app run inside your browser in a locked sandbox, never on our server. Generated images and file history stay in this browser. Safety checks run in your browser and are best-effort, not a guarantee.

Admins

Admins can switch AI models on or off. Firestore rules give them no access to your chats or files, and they cannot disable the safety checks.

Your controls

Open C-Univers-AI, then Privacy & my data. There you can delete all chats, file history, images and usage counters, choose automatic deletion of old chats (30 days, 90 days or 1 year, applied when you open the app), delete all your data, or delete your account. You can also email moonstar7023@gmail.com.

We never

sell your data. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we cannot see them.

AI safety

The AI is instructed to refuse harmful, illegal, hateful or sexual requests. This is a best-effort protection and can make mistakes. AI answers can be wrong; check important facts.

Notes rules

Upload only your own notes or material you may share. No copyrighted books, illegal or abusive content. Reported notes are removed.
