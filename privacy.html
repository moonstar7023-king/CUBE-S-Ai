Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short

Your private data (chats, projects, saved items) is stored in your own account and other users cannot read it. To get AI answers, your text is sent to outside AI services (listed below). We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. Please do not type passwords, ID numbers or bank details into the AI.

What we store

  • Login: your email address or phone number (Firebase Authentication). We cannot see your password.
  • Private to you: chat history, projects, saved library, study profile and your settings. Other users cannot read these.
  • Public to signed-in students: only the notes you choose to publish: title, text, university, subject, optional PDF/image, and your nickname (your email is not shown).
  • Reports: when someone reports a note, we keep a small record (note id, reporter id, date) to stop repeat reports.
  • On your phone: app settings, your image gallery and the offline copy of the app. You can clear them (see below).

What leaves the app (third parties)

  • AI answers, images and file analysis: your message, the text of files you attach and image descriptions are sent to Puter and to the AI companies behind the model that answers. They follow their own privacy rules. We cannot control them.
  • Search / Research: only your question words are sent to Wikipedia.
  • Fonts and code libraries: loaded from Google, cdnjs and jsDelivr, so these services can see your IP address.
  • Storage: Google Firebase stores your account data and encrypts it at rest. This is not end-to-end encryption.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or run ads.
  • We do not use your private chats to train AI ourselves.
  • We do not read your private chats. Technically, the app owner can open the database console, so we tell you this openly. Stronger protection (a private vault) is planned, not available yet.

Your controls (Settings > Privacy & data)

  • Delete all chats, or your saved library.
  • Delete your published notes (their files are deleted too).
  • Auto-delete old chats after 7, 30 or 90 days.
  • Clear this phone (settings and image gallery); you are also asked this when you log out.
  • Delete my account and all data: removes your chats, projects, library, published notes, uploaded files and login. Report records contain no content and are removed on request.

Notes rules

Upload only your own notes or material you may share. No copyrighted books, illegal or abusive content. Reported notes are hidden or removed. Be kind and use CUTE-S-AI for good.
