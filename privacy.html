Privacy Policy & Terms

Data we store: email (login), notes you choose to publish (visible to verified CUBE-S users), your name prefix shown as author.

AI chats (free default): answers are generated via Puter.js, which passes your text to AI vendors it works with. If Puter has no free credit left, the app automatically uses Pollinations (pollinations.ai) instead. The line under the chat box shows which one answered. Your chat history, projects, saved items and study profile are stored privately in your account so you can continue on any device. Only you can read them, and you can delete them anytime. When you use Search or Research, only your question words are sent to Wikipedia. Image descriptions and images you ask to read are sent to Puter.

Your own AI provider key (optional): if you connect an OpenAI, Anthropic Claude or Google Gemini key, your messages, recent chat history (up to 20 messages), text of files you attach, study profile, saved memories and project notes are sent directly from your device to that provider. That provider processes them under its own terms and privacy policy and bills your own account. Your key is encrypted on your device (AES-GCM) and is never uploaded to CUBE-S-AI, Firebase or any server of ours. You can remove a key or erase all keys anytime in Settings > AI providers. CUBE-S-AI never silently switches to a different provider if yours fails.

CUBE-S-AI Cloud (optional, only if the app owner switches it on): your messages and the text of attached files go through a Cloudflare Worker run by the app owner to the AI company you pick (OpenAI, Anthropic or Google). The Worker checks your sign-in, applies usage limits and does not store or log your messages or files. It keeps only counts (requests, tokens, cost) tied to your account, and a hashed network address counter that is deleted after 48 hours. The app tells you which company receives your text before first use.

We never sell your data or show it to third parties. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we cannot see them.

Notes rules: upload only your own notes or material you may share. No copyrighted books, illegal or abusive content. Reported notes are removed. You can delete your own notes anytime.

Helpful: CUBE-S-AI: Helpful for Everyone

Feedback: if you tap the like or dislike button, your rating, that question and that answer are sent to the CUBE-S-AI team to improve the app. Nothing is sent unless you choose to.
